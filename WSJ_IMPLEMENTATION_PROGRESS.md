### Checkout integration (19 Nov, evening)

- Catalog and legacy `Plan` checkout now look up enabled reservation policies server-side (`BillingService.reservationSelections`). When `INVENTORY_CHECKOUT_ENFORCEMENT=on` (default OFF), the order and all inventory holds (including rule-locked rooms) commit in one READ COMMITTED transaction before Stripe is contacted; the Stripe session gets `expires_at` 31 minutes ahead and the hold lasts 40 minutes. Claims link to orders via `ownerRef = order:<id>`.
- Paid webhook confirms holds under an order-row lock; expired/failed sessions, Stripe session-creation failure, unpaid cancellation and refunded cancellation release them. A payment that arrives after its hold lapsed or was released is recorded as `INVENTORY_LOST` and raises a `reservation_lost` finance alert instead of being treated as reserved. A failed release after refund raises `reservation_release_failed`.
- Fails closed for: missing time, unknown/ambiguous location, quantity > 1, recurring plans, manual payment, free totals, times without timezone, past times, and times too close to hold through payment.
- `backend/test/inventory-checkout-postgres.test.ts`: 8/8 passed on disposable PostgreSQL with fake Stripe (event race one winner, 4th room free, paid confirm + replay, expiry release, late payment flagged, 6 desk checkouts vs 5 desks -> 5, fail-closed cases, Stripe failure release, enforcement off unchanged).
