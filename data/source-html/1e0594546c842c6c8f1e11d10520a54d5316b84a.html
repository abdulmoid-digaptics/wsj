Choose the Circle that aligns with your profession or business when you join. ![](https://workspacejunction.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-2-1.png) ## What is the Workspace Junction Community Network? The Workspace Junction Community Network brings together professionals and entrepreneurs from diverse industries into one collaborative ecosystem. Members gain access to networking opportunities, professional development events, workshops, and specialized industry groups known as Circles. Each Circle focuses on a specific professional community, enabling deeper conversations, meaningful connections, and collaboration opportunities. Instead of generic networking, our model creates industry-focused communities within a larger professional network. ![](https://workspacejunction.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Rectangle-5668.png) ## How the Community Works ![](https://workspacejunction.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/communityworks1.png) ## Join the Network Become a member of the Workspace Junction Community Network and gain access to networking events, workshops, and professional resources. ![](https://workspacejunction.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/communityworks2.png) ## Choose Your Circle During checkout, select the Circle that best represents your profession, business, or industry. ![](https://workspacejunction.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/communityworks3.png) ## Connect and Collaborate Participate in events, discussions, and collaborations within your Circle and across the broader community. ## Community Circles Each Circle brings together professionals from a specific industry or professional background.
