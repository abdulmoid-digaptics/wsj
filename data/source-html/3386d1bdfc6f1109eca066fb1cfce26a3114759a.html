Professional Meeting Room Rental in
Oakbrook Terrace

Host productive meetings in a professional meeting room at Workspace Junction in Oakbrook Terrace, Illinois. Our meeting spaces are designed for business meetings, client presentations, team collaborations, and small workshops in a comfortable and professional environment.
Conveniently located near Oak Brook, Lombard, and Villa Park, our meeting rooms provide the perfect setting for entrepreneurs, remote teams, and businesses that need a professional place to meet without maintaining a full office.

Why Choose Workspace Junction Meeting Rooms

Workspace Junction provides modern meeting spaces designed to support productive conversations and professional presentations.

Benefits include:

• Professional meeting environment
• Comfortable seating for teams and clients
• High-speed WiFi
• Presentation screens for meetings and presentations
• Convenient Oakbrook Terrace location
• Flexible hourly bookings

Our meeting rooms provide the perfect balance between professionalism and flexibility.

Our Meeting Room Options

Workspace Junction offers multiple meeting room types to support different meeting styles from quick discussions to larger collaborative sessions.

Board Room

Formal settings for executive discussions, high-level decision making, and strategic planning.

Conference Room

Versatile rooms equipped for formal presentations, large gatherings, and professional meetings.

Collaboration Room

Creative spaces designed for hands-on teamwork, brainstorming, and group problem solving.

Huddle Room

Small, private areas for quick, informal chats and spontaneous team syncs.

Meeting Room Features

Workspace Junction meeting rooms offer a calm, professional environment designed for focused discussions, effective collaboration, and confident decision-making free from distractions.

Workspace Junction meeting rooms are designed to provide a calm, professional environment where teams can meet with focus, collaborate effectively, and make confident decisions.

  • Quiet, professional setting for focused discussions and collaboration
  • Flexible booking options, including hourly and half-day availability
  • Presentation-ready rooms equipped with 65″ and 85″ digital whiteboards for presentations, brainstorming, and screen sharing
  • Logitech Rally Bar and Logitech Tap for one-touch meetings on Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet™
  • High-speed internet for seamless in-person and hybrid meetings
  • Comfortable seating and thoughtfully designed room layouts
  • Access to shared amenities and on-site support Client-ready spaces that reflect your professionalism

No. Meeting rooms at Workspace Junction can be booked by both members and non-members. Members may receive preferred access or special rates.

Yes, our private rooms can be booked for specific times. They are ideal for confidential calls, online meetings, or uninterrupted work.
