Just a place to sit, read, and focus. ## Visit the Library Become a member Looking for a quiet place to read or explore information? ## How It Works Looking for a quiet place to read or explore information? - Become a Workspace Junction Member Library access is available to Workspace Junction members with an active **Coworking Membership** or **Dedicated Desk Membership**. - Create Your Library User Profile Once your membership is active, our team creates your **Library User profile** (or activates it) so you can check out materials under your name. - Browse the Library Collection Explore the curated catalog on-site and select the books you’d like to check out. - Check Out Your Books at the Front Desk Bring your selected books to the front desk. We’ll **scan and assign** the items to your Library User profile and confirm the due date. - Enjoy Your Reading Time Read on-site in the library area or take your books with you (based on the item’s lending rules). - Return or Renew Return items by the due date at the front desk. If you need more time, you can request a **renewal** (subject to availability and lending limits). ![](https://workspacejunction.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/library-image3.png) ## Quiet Resource for Members The Workspace Junction Library is a curated resource space designed for our members and resident professionals. To maintain a focused and productive environment, library access is reserved for Workspace Junction members and authorized guests. This space reflects our commitment to supporting learning, research, and thoughtful work within our professional community. Who can access the Workspace Junction Library? The Workspace Junction Library is available to **members with active Coworking or Dedicated Memberships**. Access is designed to support learning, research, and personal development within our professional community. Do I need to register to borrow books? Yes. Members must have a **Library User profile** created by our team in order to check out books from the library. Can I take books outside the library? Yes. Members may check out selected books through the front desk and take them with them according to the library’s lending guidelines. How do I check out a book? Simply bring your selected book to the **front desk**, where it will be assigned to your Library User profile and a return date will be provided. How long can I keep a borrowed book? Loan periods may vary depending on the material. The return date will be shared when the book is checked out. Can I renew a book if I need more time? Yes. Members may request a **renewal**, provided the book has not been requested by another member. Can non-members access the library? Library access is **reserved for Workspace Junction members and authorized users** to maintain a quiet and focused environment. What types of materials are available in the library? The Workspace Junction Library includes **curated books and research catalogues focused on business, entrepreneurship, personal growth, technology, and professional development**. Can I read in the library without checking out a book? Yes. Members are welcome to **read and study in the library space** without borrowing materials. Who should I contact if I need help finding a book? Our team at the **Workspace Junction front desk** is happy to assist members with locating materials or answering library questions.
