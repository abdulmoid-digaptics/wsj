What types of break areas are available at WorkSpace Junction? Workspace Junction provides well-furnished common rooms where you can take short breaks, have coffee, or relax in a professional setting. For additional privacy, there are two private rooms available for phone calls, focused work, or quiet moments Can I reserve a private room for calls or meetings? Yes, our private rooms can be booked for specific times. They are ideal for confidential calls, online meetings, or uninterrupted work. Are refreshments allowed in common and private rooms? Yes, you are welcome to bring coffee and light refreshments into the common rooms and private rooms while respecting the comfort and cleanliness of the workspace. How do I book a private room? You can reserve a private room through our online booking system, or by contacting our staff for available time slots.
