What types of events does Workspace Junction host? Workspace Junction hosts a variety of professional and community events including workshops, networking events, panel discussions, training sessions, startup meetups, and educational seminars. Many of our events focus on entrepreneurship, technology, creativity, and community building. Are events free for Workspace Junction members? Yes. Many of our events are **free for Workspace Junction members**. Some events may be **exclusive to members only**, while others are open to the public with a ticket fee for non-members. Can non-members attend events? Yes. Many events are open to the public. Non-members may attend by **purchasing a ticket** through our event registration page unless the event is marked **Members-Only**. How do I register for an event? You can register directly through the **Events page on our website**. Each event includes a registration link and details about pricing, capacity, and schedule. What happens if an event is full? Events may have limited seating. Once capacity is reached, registration will close. In some cases, a **waitlist option** may be available. Do members need to register for free events? Yes. Even if an event is free for members, we ask that you **register in advance** so we can properly manage seating and logistics. Are food and refreshments provided at events? Some events include refreshments or light snacks. Details will be listed in the event description. Can I bring a guest to a members-only event? Members-only events are typically reserved for members. However, some events may allow **member guests**. Please check the event details. Can I host my own event at Workspace Junction? Yes. Workspace Junction offers event space rental for workshops, seminars, networking events, and private gatherings. What is the capacity of the event space? Our event space can accommodate **up to 50 guests**, depending on the seating layout and event format. What types of events can be hosted at Workspace Junction? You can host a variety of events including: - Workshops - Business trainings - Networking events - Meetups - Community gatherings - Panel discussions - Educational seminars - Small private events What amenities are included when renting the event space? Our event space includes: - Flexible seating arrangements - High-speed WiFi - Presentation screen / TV - Whiteboards - Comfortable professional environment - Optional coworking access for guests Additional services may be available upon request. How long can I rent the event space for? Event rentals are available **hourly or for half-day and full-day bookings**, depending on availability. How far in advance should I book the space? We recommend booking **at least 1–2 weeks in advance**, especially for evenings or weekends. Can Workspace Junction help promote my event? Yes. Depending on the event, we may help promote it through: - Our community newsletter - Social media channels - Member network This is evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Can I bring my own catering or food? Yes. You may bring outside catering or refreshments. Please coordinate with our team beforehand. How do I request to rent the event space? You can submit a request through our **Event Space Booking form** on the website or contact our team directly. Is there a discount for Workspace Junction members? Yes. **Members may receive discounted pricing** for event space rentals. Can I tour the space before booking? Absolutely. You are welcome to **schedule a tour** to see the event space and discuss your event needs.
