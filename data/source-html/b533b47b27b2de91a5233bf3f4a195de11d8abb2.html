A **[dedicated desk](https://workspacejunction.com/dedicated)** is a reserved workstation within a coworking environment that belongs exclusively to you. Unlike hot desks or open seating arrangements, your workspace remains available whenever you arrive. You can leave your monitor, documents, office supplies, and personal equipment at your desk without needing to set up every day. This creates a consistent and professional work environment while still giving you access to all the benefits of a coworking community.
