What is a Notary Public? A Notary Public is an independent official who verifies identities, witnesses signatures, and certifies documents so they are legally valid. Why do documents need notarization? Some legal and official documents won’t be accepted by courts, banks, or government offices unless they bear a notary’s seal confirming authenticity. Do I need an appointment? It’s recommended to book in advance to ensure availability and reduce your waiting time. Can I notarize digital or online documents? Yes — but most authorities still require a printed copy with signatures witnessed by the notary.
