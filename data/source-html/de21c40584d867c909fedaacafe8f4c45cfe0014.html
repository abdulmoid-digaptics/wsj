What types of break areas are available at WorkSpace Junction? WorkSpace Junction provides well-furnished common rooms where you can take short breaks, have coffee, or relax in a professional setting. For additional privacy, there are two private rooms available for phone calls, focused work, or quiet moments. Can I reserve a private room for calls or meetings? Yes, our private rooms can be booked for specific times. They are ideal for confidential calls, online meetings, or uninterrupted work. Are the common rooms meant for socializing? The common rooms at WorkSpace Junction are designed primarily for relaxation and short breaks—not for large gatherings or group activities. These spaces maintain a professional, quiet atmosphere. Are refreshments allowed in common and private rooms? Yes, you are welcome to bring coffee and light refreshments into the common rooms and private rooms while respecting the comfort and cleanliness of the workspace. How do I book a private room? You can reserve a private room through our online booking system, or by contacting our staff for available time slots. Is there a time limit for using the common rooms? Common rooms are available for short breaks throughout the workday. We ask all guests to be mindful of others and limit extended stays to ensure everyone can enjoy the space.
